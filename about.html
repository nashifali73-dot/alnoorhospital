Our Story

From Humble Beginnings to a Trusted Healthcare Institution

Al Noor Hospital was founded in the early 2000s by the visionary businessman Mr. Bava Haji, with a simple promise: world-class care, close to home.

Under his leadership the hospital has earned the trust of the local community and beyond — known for renowned doctors, an efficient and dedicated staff, and an unwavering focus on patient well-being.

Today, our success is measured not only by our facilities and technology, but by the positive outcomes and experiences of every patient we serve.

Our Mission

To provide expert, compassionate and affordable healthcare that puts every patient's well-being first.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted name in healthcare for Tirur and the Malappuram district.
