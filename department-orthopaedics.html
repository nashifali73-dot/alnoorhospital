Overview

Helping You Move Freely Again

The Orthopaedic Department combines medical expertise with compassionate care for bone, joint and musculoskeletal conditions. Whether it's a sports injury, a fracture or long-standing joint pain, our specialists focus on accurate diagnosis and a treatment plan that fits your life.

Frequently Asked Questions
Do I need a referral to see an orthopaedic doctor?

No. You can book an OP appointment directly online or by calling the front desk.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Bring any previous X-rays, scan reports, prescriptions and a list of current medicines.

What if I have an injury outside OP hours?

Come straight to our 24/7 Emergency department or call 9562 9000 10.
