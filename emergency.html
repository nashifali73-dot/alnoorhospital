When to Come

Signs You Need Emergency Care

If you or someone near you shows any of these signs, don't wait — call us or come to the Emergency department straight away.

Call 9562 9000 10
  • Chest pain or pressure
  • Difficulty breathing
  • Sudden weakness, slurred speech or facial drooping
  • Severe bleeding or serious injury
  • Loss of consciousness or seizures
  • High fever with drowsiness in children
  • Severe burns, poisoning or allergic reaction
  • Severe abdominal pain
